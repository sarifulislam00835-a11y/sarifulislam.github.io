Digitalization and Preservation of Indigenous Cultural Identity in Bodoland

This research examines the impact of rapid digitalization on indigenous cultural identity in Bodoland, with particular attention to the challenges and opportunities it creates for preserving culture, traditions, language, history, and community knowledge.
