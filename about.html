ABOUT ME

Sariful Islam

Writer • Researcher • Educator

Sariful Islam is a student, writer and emerging researcher from Assam, India. Born on 13 April 2005 in Sapkata, Kokrajhar, his interests span education, literature, culture and research. Beyond academics, he actively engages in writing, debates, youth programmes and academic initiatives. Driven by curiosity and a belief in learning beyond the classroom, he aims to use knowledge, research and creative expression to understand society and contribute meaningfully to it.
